Feather Baker

This tool bakes NormalizedCrow's feather and fur shader to a normal unity mesh.

  • Looks and works very close to the unbaked shader.
  • Improves GPU preformance by 2-5x.
  • Makes it work in fallback!
Drawbacks/Considerations before use:
  • 2-4x mesh file size.
  • Feathers now eat into the VRC polygon budget.
  • Extra bake step needed after grooming.

If you encounter bugs with the tool, or something not working right, feel free to poke @Torvid on telegram ^^. I can't guarantee 100% availability, but making the tool less buggy would be nice.

Download

1. FeathersAndFurBakeMeshV4.unitypackage

Baking

  1. Import the package.
  2. In the top panel, Go to Tools->Torvid->FeathersAndFur Bake Mesh.
  3. In the window, assign in your avatar's Skinned Mesh Renderer from the Scene.
  4. Click "Bake Cards" (generates a copy of the mesh and a new material with the shader swapped)
  5. Click "Assign Baked" (swaps out the mesh and material for the baked ones)

FBX Export

  1. In the top panel, Go to Tools->Torvid->Export FBX
  2. Install the required unity package if you don't already have it.
  3. In the window, assign in your avatar's Root Object from the Scene.
  4. Click Export!
